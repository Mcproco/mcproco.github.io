Reflection

What an assignment. Though lengthy, and pretty heavy on the terms and requirements, it was probably the most fun school project I've ever done. My topic, though non tangible, forced me to actually work hard on a program to try and get it done. Though all FOSS projects are never finished, I would say this one is without a doubt unfinished. There was so much work involved to get to the point where its at.

There were a multitude of moments where I had fun, was completely stuck, or was so tired that after reading a sentence into some math PDF I'd just fall asleep on the spot.

Though, alot of this year has just been me not being able to focus (or even just being efficient for that matter), and I've been trying to work on that.

If I could do this all over again, I'd for sure plan things out more thoughtfully, and actually follow those plans. There were a lot of instances where I'd plan to get some set of tasks out of the way, but either there would be some unpredictible responsibility I'd have to take care of, or I would get destracted with something irrelevant. Of course, I'd feel bad at the end of the day for not finishing anything, but that made up a lot of nights.

Looking back on it, its kind of surprising that I got as far as I did in that project. As a matter of fact, a lot of those concepts that I used are taught in college for thousands of dollars in tuition (Linear Algebra, Graphics Programming, etc).

This project's scant visual result I feel unfortunately reflects how I've been since forever. Many times do I undergo internal conflict or struggle on some assignment or project, just for it to be alright enough work in the end. This project has hit that fact in my face the hardest.


I'd want to tell you that I'm satisfied with my project. I'd be complacent for saying so however.


It simply didn't reach my expectations. I expected myself to get some impressive project shoveled out. That didn't happen. Although my project can still have an impact, it by no means is life changing.


For any of you future seniors, though

If you're reading this, then going the extra mile for this overkill project and website was worth it. If I was happy with anything out of this project, it was doing something unique and going full throttle with it. (And finally realizing the value of time and time management!!!) Once you get that green light for your project, take your ideas and work hard to make them reality.

Just don't make the same mistakes that I've made. I want you to make sure that:

You've got realistic expectations

You plan, plan and plan ahead.

You forgive yourself when you mess up.

You prioritize your goals and everyday tasks

You manage your time properly to work on the Senior Project and any other responsibilities you may have

Oh, and stop using social media. I hate to sound like your mom but its true. You only realize how many days you've wasted once you realize you don't have time left...

ANYWAYS HAVE FUN WITH YOUR SENIOR YEAR!!!!
